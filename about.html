Our school

About Wolmer’s

Wolmer’s Trust High School for Boys is located at National Heroes Circle, Kingston 4. Its motto, Age Quod Agis, encourages students to give their best to whatever they do.

School history

1729
John Wolmer’s will endowed a free school.
1896
The Boys’ and Girls’ divisions became separate schools.
1909
New buildings opened at Quebec Lodge following the 1907 earthquake.
1984
The joint sixth-form block opened for the Boys’ and Girls’ schools.
2015
The Douglas Orane Auditorium was dedicated.

Academics and school life

The school emphasizes academic achievement, athletics and community involvement. Its activities include a boys’ choir and a house system with Crosse, Wolfe, Harrison and Patterson houses.
